# M07 Motivation — Product Requirements

## Motivation

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-52 | The system shall show a short encouragement message when a user completes a task. | Keeps users motivated. | High |
| FR-53 | The system shall send a nudge when a task becomes overdue or is due within 24 hours with no scheduled time. | Catches tasks before they slip further. | Medium |
| FR-54 | The system shall send a morning summary of the day’s tasks and overdue tasks at a time chosen by the user. | Gives a daily overview without opening the app. | Medium |
| FR-55 | The system shall allow a user to turn encouragement messages, nudges, and the morning summary on or off separately. | Not every user wants every message. | Low |
