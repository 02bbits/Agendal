# M06 Inbox & Views — Product Requirements

## Inbox View

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-43 | The system shall open the Inbox View as the home screen after login. | Today is the most common need. | High |
| FR-44 | The Inbox View shall list tasks that are due or scheduled today. | Shows what needs attention today. | High |
| FR-45 | The Inbox View shall show overdue tasks first, in a separate section. | Prevents missed tasks from being overlooked. | High |
| FR-46 | The Inbox View shall show the day’s progress at the top as completed tasks out of today’s total. | Motivates users as they work through the day. | High |
| FR-47 | The system shall allow a user to mark a task as complete from the Inbox View. | Lets users work through the day in one view. | High |
| FR-48 | The Inbox View shall order today’s tasks by scheduled time, then by priority. | Puts the next thing to do at the top. | Medium |

## Upcoming and Projects lists

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-49 | The system shall provide an Upcoming list of tasks due or scheduled after today, grouped by date. | Supports planning ahead. | High |
| FR-50 | The system shall provide a Projects list that shows each project and its tasks. | Shows work grouped by goal. | High |
| FR-51 | The system shall show a change made in any view or by the assistant in all other views without the user reloading the app. | Prevents conflicting information. | High |
